# DSES Trademark and Naming Policy

"DSES", "Decision-Sequence Evidence Schema", and "Evidify" are claimed as
trademarks of Joshua M. Henderson / Evidify LLC. The specification is CC BY
4.0 and the code is MIT; this policy governs the names, not the technology.

## Permitted without permission

- Truthful reference: "implements DSES", "based on DSES", "exports DSES
  packages", academic citation, criticism, commentary.
- **DSES-compatible**: self-declared by an implementer who believes their
  implementation interoperates. No verification required; the claim is the
  implementer's own.

## Requires the conformance process

- **DSES Conformant**: only per CONFORMANCE-POLICY.md (published gate passed,
  transcript in the registry, version named).
- Future certification tiers (independent assessment, profile-specific marks
  such as an Outcome Layer mark) are reserved and not yet offered; do not use
  "DSES Certified" for anything today.

## Not permitted

Using the names in a product name, company name, or domain in a way that
implies origin from or endorsement by the maintainer; modifying the
specification and distributing it under the same name (call it a derivative
and rename it); using the marks on a nonconforming implementation described
as conformant.
